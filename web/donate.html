Help now

Keep the world running.

Sim Horde is free, has no ads and never will. One person builds and runs it, and the shared world lives on a single computer that has to stay on day and night. If you'd like to help in a way other than lending your device, a donation goes straight to keeping it online and growing.

Where it goes

An always-on server
so the world keeps turning even when the computer it lives on today is off or travelling.
A signed installer
so Windows stops warning people that the desktop app is from an unknown publisher.
The domain and the rest
sim-horde.org, backups, and the time it takes to build what's next.

Good to know

  • Donations go to the person who runs Sim Horde, not to a charity, so they aren't tax-deductible.
  • Giving doesn't change anything in the simulation or on the leaderboard: everyone's device counts the same.
  • Payments are handled by the payment provider. Sim Horde never sees your card details.
  • Lending your phone, tablet or computer helps just as much. Help from this device.
