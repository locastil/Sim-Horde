What if…?

An experimental sandbox: set up what happens, where and how hard, then watch the whole planet play it out on your device, month by month.

Nothing you set up here touches the shared world, and nothing is sent anywhere. It's a simulation built on real country statistics, not a forecast.

Describe your world

The scenario assistant turns what you write into a plan you can check before anything runs. It works in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere. It knows a set list of events, places and timings, so it will say when it doesn't follow.
